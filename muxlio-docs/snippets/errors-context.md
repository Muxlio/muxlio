    Muxlio is a durable workflow **coordinator** (immutable **plans**, durable **runs**, **workers** and **adapters**). See [What Muxlio is](../index.md#what-is-muxlio), [Start Muxlio](../service.md), and [Setup defaults](../service.md#setup-defaults).
